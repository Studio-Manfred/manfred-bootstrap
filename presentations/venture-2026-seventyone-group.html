Section 06 · Risks

The risks we know about.

We wrote a CHALLENGE-ME.md doc that argues against the venture. The strongest challenges:

  • The 2.5-hour proof was in our own perfect environment, not a messy client codebase.
  • "AI product ops as a service" is a category we invented — market doesn't use this vocabulary yet.
  • The Manfred brand is Jens-shaped; founder-brand consultancies hit a ceiling.
  • Cursor + Windsurf now belong to Big Tech — a competing "team process layer" could arrive in 12 months.
  • Shared-IP model is a fundraising blocker if we ever want institutional capital.
  • 25% of enterprise AI spend has been postponed to 2027 — Q4 pilot budgets may be tight.

We are not hiding these. They're in the docs. Ask questions.
