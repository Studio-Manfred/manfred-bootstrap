## Slide 10 — Where this venture fits into the SeventyOne Group

**Three plausible structures:**

**1. Inside the Group — as a fourth firm**
A new entity co-owned by the three current firms + Jens as operating founder. Positioned as the AI-first delivery arm of the Group. Each firm contributes distinct capabilities.

**2. Alongside the Group — as a peer**
Standalone company Jens builds, using the Group's brands and networks as go-to-market accelerants but with independent equity and ops. Some upside sharing (advisor / referral fee) but no shared IP.

**3. Outside the Group — as a spinout**
Jens leaves Studio Manfred to build the venture separately. Clean equity story for investors; heaviest personal risk; loses group-network leverage.

**My recommendation: Option 1 (inside the Group as a fourth firm)** — because the capabilities we each bring are genuinely complementary and none of us alone would build this as well as the three of us together.

**Speaker notes:** _I have a real preference for structure 1 — but only if it works for everyone. This is the slide where we should actually have the conversation, not just present it. Structure 3 is my backup if structure 1 doesn't work for the group._
